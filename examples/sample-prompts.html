Sample Prompts

Batch triage

Triage these three intake items as a batch. Classify each as reject, hold, clarify, business case, charter, or portfolio scoring candidate. Show readiness score, key gaps, and downstream route.

Billing workflow fix

A CFO says billing exceptions are delaying close. Finance has exception counts and manual rework examples. The billing platform owner and data team would be involved. Determine whether this should become a business case, charter, or scoring candidate.

Platform modernization

A CTO wants to modernize a legacy platform. Engineering says support risk is rising, but cost, funding, operational impact, and timing are unclear. Triage readiness and recommend the next step.

AI automation idea

A department lead wants an AI assistant to draft customer follow-up notes. There is no approved tool, privacy review, baseline, or sponsor beyond the department lead. Triage the request and identify the correct route.
