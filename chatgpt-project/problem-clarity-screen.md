# Problem Clarity Screen

## Purpose

Separate real business problems from solution requests, preferences, noise, and unsupported urgency.

## Questions

- What problem is being solved?
- Who experiences the problem?
- What evidence shows the problem exists?
- What is the current workaround or failure mode?
- What measurable outcome is expected?
- Why now?
- What happens if no action is taken?

## Red flags

- Starts with a tool, vendor, feature, platform, or technology choice.
- Uses broad claims such as efficiency, modernization, visibility, or automation without evidence.
- No affected process, customer, team, or control is named.
- Outcome is activity rather than result.

## Output

State the clearest evidence-bound problem statement. If one cannot be created, classify the item as clarify and list missing evidence.
