# Sponsorship Screen

## Purpose

Determine whether the request has an accountable sponsor with decision authority, funding influence, and willingness to own the outcome.

## Questions

- Who is the accountable sponsor?
- Is the sponsor different from the requester?
- Has the sponsor confirmed the request?
- What decision can the sponsor make?
- Does the sponsor own funding, policy, operational outcome, or executive escalation?
- What happens if the sponsor does not act?

## Signals of weak sponsorship

- Requester says leadership wants it but cannot name an accountable leader.
- Sponsor is a group, committee, or department rather than a person.
- Funding, capacity, or decision rights are unknown.
- No one can explain the decision being requested.

## Output

Classify sponsorship as clear, usable with confirmation, weak, absent, or inappropriate. List the minimum sponsor action needed before routing.
