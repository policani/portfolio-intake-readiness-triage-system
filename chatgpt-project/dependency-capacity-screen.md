# Dependency and Capacity Screen

## Purpose

Identify whether the request is blocked by unknown dependencies, capacity constraints, timing conflicts, or system ownership issues.

## Review areas

- Impacted teams
- Impacted systems
- Vendor or third-party dependency
- Data dependency
- Security, privacy, legal, finance, audit, or compliance review
- Release calendar or blackout window
- Budget or funding source
- Resource availability
- Executive decision timing
- Relationship to existing initiatives

## Flags

- Unknown capacity
- Unfunded mandate
- Cross-functional dependency
- System owner not identified
- Compliance review required
- Vendor dependency
- Timing conflict
- Duplicate or overlapping work

## Output

List flags, likely blockers, and what must be confirmed before the item can move downstream.
